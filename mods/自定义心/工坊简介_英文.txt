[h2]What is this[/h2]
A DIY Limbus Company buff for Depersonalization: Shin (the "heart" buff).

[h2]Warning: If you have other mods equipped that use hotkeys, it is recommended to copy them in after completing the input externally; otherwise, problems may occur!!! The game's own hotkeys are not affected at present.[/h2]

[list]
[*] Everything for Shin is included in this package: the default Shin buff (880901), the trait, the icon and the frame-animation effect
[*] Requires the BepInEx framework mod
[*] In the lobby, open "Interlude Time" and choose "Edit this investigator's Shin buff", then hit Save
[/list]

[h2]What you can customize[/h2]
[list]
[*] Speed is a fixed entry, up to 100
[*] Up to 4 more entries: Damage Bonus (max 5), Damage Reduction (max 2), Dodge, Willpower, Occultism, Brawl, Shooting, Athletics (max 50 each), Physical/Magic Damage Reduction % (max 50%), Physical/Magic Damage Bonus % (max 100%)
[*] The name is always "Shin (investigator) - suffix"; you pick the suffix, duplicate names are rejected
[*] Costs 1 SP per round; removed when SP falls into Weakness/Collapse or when the battle ends
[/list]

Can't decide? Don't worry - this mod also comes with a handy random roll feature!

[h2]How the random roll works[/h2]
[list]
[*] One d100 (1-100) is rolled per entry, and the number is converted into the value
[*] Speed: the roll itself
[*] Damage Bonus: roll / 20, rounded up
[*] Damage Reduction: roll / 50, rounded up
[*] Other stats: roll / 2, rounded up
[*] Physical / Magic Damage Bonus %: the roll itself
[*] Physical / Magic Damage Reduction %: roll / 2, rounded up
[/list]
Rounding rules:
[list]
[*] Except Damage Bonus / Damage Reduction, results are rounded to the nearest multiple of 5
[*] Percentage entries are rounded to the nearest multiple of 10
[*] Values are never 0: normal entries min 5, Damage Bonus / Reduction min 1, percentages min 10%
[/list]
Each reroll costs 5 Luck.

Investigators without a custom Shin simply use the default "Shin" buff.

[h2]Recent additions[/h2]
[list]
[*] When the Shin trait is asleep you no longer gain Shin automatically at the start of a battle. Instead you get the skill "Activate Shin": +50 Speed for the round (to seize the initiative); after the action, spend 5 SP to gain Shin and 4 Armor. That Armor ("Poised") is removed at the start of the next round
[*] Three interlude entries now: "Edit this investigator's Shin buff" / "Add Shin" / "Remove Shin"
[/list]

[h2]This update[/h2]
Finally pulled it off: by feeding the first and last frames to an AI video generator, the author now has a complete AI-made frame sequence for the effect. The result is close to 90% of a faithful recreation of Limbus Company's Shin. Rejoice!
