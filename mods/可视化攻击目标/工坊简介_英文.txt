[h2]What is this[/h2]
Visualizes who is attacking whom: every action that has been locked in gets an arrow from that character's action slot to the target it will act on.

[h2]How to read the arrows[/h2]
[list]
[*] Dashed line: AOE (area / random target)
[*] Solid line: single target
[*] Red: aimed at enemies (throw actions are always red)
[*] Green: aimed at allies
[*] Blue: interference actions (the vanilla non-damaging skills aimed at enemies)
[*] Yellow: clash (two characters targeting each other)
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*] Requires the BepInEx framework mod
[*] Arrows only appear during the action-selection phase, and are all cleared once the round starts resolving
[*] Arrows are a forecast: if the target dies, gets intercepted or covered, or is grappled before the action resolves, the actual target may change
[/list]

This build is visualization only. More iterations are on the way — stay tuned.
