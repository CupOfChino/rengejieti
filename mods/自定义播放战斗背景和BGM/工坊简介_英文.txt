Custom Battle Background & BGM

==========================================================
Description（工坊简介正文，从这里往下整段复制）
==========================================================
This mod lets you switch battle backgrounds and BGM. In battle you can switch to a background and music you prepared in advance — keep the battlefield on your own rhythm.

And most importantly of all, it can play "Shinjuku Showdown - Rain Love ver." (here comes the dumpling vinegar)

[h2]How to use[/h2]
To keep this package clean, the resources above are not included, but setting up your own is very easy. Prepare your ogg / mp3 / mp4 / png / jpg files, then in the interlude pick the custom domain action, click "choose file" and pick your resource. The mod copies it into the mod folder automatically — no manual file management needed.

[h2]Important[/h2]
If you unsubscribe from this mod, remember to manually clean up the asset folder at %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\MeowNature\Depersonalization-Release\CustomBattleBg !!! (paste that path straight into your file explorer's address bar)
